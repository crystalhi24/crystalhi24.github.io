Poisonous Plants

Poison Ivy (in notes might be called as PI for short)

Poison Ivy has Urushiol: Leads to Allergic Reaction

  • urushiol: oily resin in leaves, stems, and roots of poison ivy & poison oak & poison sumac
  • 15%- 25% of people don't get allergic reaction

Symptoms

  • Redness
  • Itching
  • Swelling
  • Blisters
  • Difficulty breathing (if inhale smoke from burning PI)

What does the Rash Look Like?

  • Straight line, the way plant brushes against skin
  • Spread out, if the contact came from something else that touched PI (ex: clothing, pet fur)
  • Across body, if oil transferred to other parts of body through fingers

What does PI Look Like?

  • Grow on small plants / vines
  • Clusters of leaves, each containing three leaflets
  • May-July: clusters of small greenish flowers & white berries

Reaction Developing Time: 12-48 hr after exposure. Can last 2-3 weeks

How to Heal

  • *****DO NOT SCRATCH
  • *****GO SEE A DOCTOR (especially if inhaled smoke from burned PI)
  • Wash skin w/ soap & water
  • Apply soothing lotions & oral antihistamines
  • Apply cold wet cloth for 15-30 min a day
  • Baths w/ baking soda
