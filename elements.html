Deadly Elements

Beryllium (in notes might be called BE for short)

Beryllium: 4th element, symbol Be, mineral found in nature

Industrial Purposes

  • Aerospace
  • Aircraft manufacture & maintenance
  • Computer
  • Dental laboratories
  • Telecommunications
  • Foundries & metal reclamation

Health Effects (not everyone gets them)

  • Once someone has been exposed to BE, they have lifelong risk of developing disease even after exposure stops
  • BE sensitization: hypersensitivity to BE
  • Chronic BE disease (CBD): scarring lung disorder, scarring prevents oxygen moving freely
  • Lung cancer
  • Acute BE disease: pneumonia-like illness, uncommon
  • Skin diseases: allergic reaction w/ rash

Symptoms of Having Health Effects (go see a doctor if you have one of these)

  • Chest pain
  • Fever
  • Increased coughing, difficulty breathing
  • Increased fatigue
  • Short breath on exertion
  • Swollen feet
  • Weight loss

Treatment: stop exposure
