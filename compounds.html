Destructive Compounds

Cyanide (in notes might be called CY for short)

What's in CY: group of compounds with highly reactive cyanide anion

Where to find CY? Produced naturally in...

  • Plants
  • Fungi
  • Bacteria

Forms of CY

  • Hydrogen CY: colorless gas
  • Sodium & Potassium CY: white/colorless solids

How to recognize CY: faint almond odor (bitter almond taste)

Exposure of CY

  • Eat food (ex: pits/seeds of common fruits)containing CY
  • Drink water containing CY
  • Breathe contaminated air
  • Smoking / secondhanded smoke

Health Effects

  • Cause death if high levels of CY in air, food, drink, or on skin
  • Breathing difficulty
  • Nervous system changes (dizziness, headache)
  • Impaired thyroid gland function
  • Permanent impairments resembling Parkinson's

CY Treatment: CY antidote
