Venoms From Animals

Pufferfish: Tetrodotoxin (in notes might be called TT for short)

Tetrodotoxin found in liver organs of some species of...

  • Fish
  • Amphibian
  • Octopus
  • Shellfish

How Human Poisoning Occur

  • Organs of the fish improperly prepared
  • TT interferes signals from nerves -> muscles
  • TT poisoning can be fatal

Poisoning Time Course

  • Rapid onset: 10-45 min
  • Delayed onset: 3-6 hr
  • Death occurrence: 20 min-24 hr

Symptoms

  • Paralysis of muscles (ex: in respiratory tract, can lead to death)
  • Numbness
  • Tingling sensation
  • Headache
  • Vomiting
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Abnormal heart rhythm
  • Low blood pressure

Treatment

  • No known antidotes / antitoxins
  • Treat symptomatically
  • Small amounts of pain relief
